# Source-aware parallel reading

Import legally supplied synthetic text as an edition of an immutable normalized
representation. Admit source spans only through its checked `EvidenceSelector`,
then keep witness, context, conflicting interpretations, reflection, and
response as separate annotations. Quotation resolves the admitted span and
checks edition expiry and licence ceilings.

Removing the edition removes its spans and leaves their annotations explicitly
orphaned. Rebuilding derived indexes cannot attach those annotations to a new
edition, even when substitute text looks the same. This recipe performs no
fetch, search, capture, provider-snippet matching, or model citation lookup.
